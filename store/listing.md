```text
The forest is full of little birds waiting to find their flock. In Fly Home, a few thoughtful taps can turn a crowded branch into a happy homecoming.

Tap a branch to pick up the matching birds at its tip, then move them to an empty branch or one topped with the same kind. Bring a full flock together and watch it fly into its birdhouse. Every open perch gives you room to plan the next move.

Explore 210 puzzles across 10 painted forest worlds, from bamboo groves and snowy woods to blossom-filled trees and wisteria. Sleeping birds, eggs, crows and unusual branches change how you plan a move.

Aim for three stars with careful moves. Undo a move when you need to rethink, or use tools such as hints, an extra branch and a wake-up bell. Returning flocks can help their neighbors, so choosing who goes home first matters too.

Meet the birds in your collection, choose their outfits and try different birdhouse styles. Collect stars to open reward chests, and return for daily gifts.

A little color, a little planning, and one more flock on its way home.
```
