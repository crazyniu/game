## Design decisions (mine, follow them exactly; sizes are design px on 1080x1920)
- **Visual language**: light, calm, clean "cream glass" UI: soft cream rounded squares (radius 28) with a thin warm-brown 3 px outline, a soft 6 px drop shadow, subtle top highlight, NO glossy orange wood, NO thick bevels. Icons are COLORED pictograms with a dark-brown 3 px outline (not white silhouettes). Accent colors: leaf green for primary/positive, warm amber for coins, soft red only for badges. Numbers in Fredoka bold, dark brown.
- **Top row** (one slim row, centre y=100, nothing else above the board but the goal tray): coin chip left (coin icon 48 + number, chip 190x72, left edge 40); level badge centre (a 72x72 leaf-shaped green badge with the level number); settings right (80x80 cream rounded square, small gear icon, right edge 1040). Gaps between elements >= 60.
- **Goal tray replaces the big hanging birdhouses**: ONE cream plank 920x150 centered at y=250, hanging from the top canopy on two short ropes. On it, one **goal chip per species** (K chips, evenly spaced; K up to 7 must fit: chip 104x120 each): a small tinted birdhouse icon (roof colored by the species) with the species' face peeking from the door, and 4 small progress dots under it (filled = birds already home... flocks fly into the chip; dots show how many of the 4 are in the box once homed it shows a green check). A 34 px gift badge (tiny icon in a circle) sits on the chip's top-right corner. Press-and-hold a chip shows an info card (bird portrait, name, gift icon + one short line of text). Provide the K=3 version and the K=7 version.
- **Song meter**: a slim pill 360x48 centered at y=1590 just above the bottom bar: 5 small notes (empty outline / filled gold). Nothing else in that row.
- **Bottom bar**: up to 4 buttons, 116x116 cream rounded squares at y=1745, centers x = 225/435/645/855, count badges 40 px at the top-right corner. Pictograms must be literal and readable at 64 px: Undo = curved back arrow; Hint = glowing light bulb; Add branch = a small branch with a green plus; Peek-eggs (Owl Eye) = an egg with an eye above it; Wake (Morning Bell) = a bell with sun rays; Scarecrow = a crow with a red cross; Spring rain = a rain cloud over a sprout. Disabled = same button at 45 percent. At 0 stock the badge becomes a green "+".
- **Board**: playable band y 420..1520. Branches: left branches span x 0..520, right branches x 560..1080, so tips never touch (>= 60 px gap), rows alternate with a vertical offset of 60. Bush at each root. Birds as in the current art.
- **Panels**: win panel (cream panel 820x760, leaf-green ribbon header with a small trophy icon, 3 big stars, coin reward chip, three buttons: replay small, NEXT large green primary, home small), settings panel (sound toggle and restart, close), booster-unlock card (shows the new booster pictogram big, its name in one short line, what it does in one short line, a green "got it" check button).
