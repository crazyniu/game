# Purchase button placement

The Shop booster/nest Buy and Aviary outfit Buy now sit below the unchanged modal frame with a 48 px gap (`Modal.ACTION_DROP`). Their complete existing Button nodes move, including their art, painted price and hit areas. Shop Buy remains 560 × 132; outfit Buy remains 480 × 132. Their horizontal centers remain at x = 540 on the 1080 × 1920 canvas.

This adjustment deliberately preserves the panel and all existing content positions. Quantity controls remain where they were. Purchase Preview and all other dialogs are managed separately. No artwork or text texture is changed.
