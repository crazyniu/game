# Hundred levels: spec

User request (2026-11-09): at least 100 levels, difficulty rising from simple to complex; the plan was approved with
new painted forests and the "40 levels" project rule changed to 100.

- 100 levels, 10 chapters of 10, one forest per chapter (Green, Bamboo, Autumn, Snow, Blossom, Mushroom, Lakeside, Misty
  Pine, Rainforest, Wisteria).
- All 40 authored levels stay; their saved stars follow them to their new numbers.
- One new mechanic per chapter from chapter 2: sleepers, mama and chicks, crows and twigs, swivel, growing, day and night.
- Score: `0.6 * counted moves + colours + 10 * (1 - random win rate) + mechanics`.
- Chapter averages rise; each new mechanic's first level stays short so it can teach.
