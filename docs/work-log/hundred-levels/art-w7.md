## Checks and remaining deviations

All ten outputs were visually inspected. Pillow verified dimensions and modes. All six transparent pictures have genuine alpha; small magenta fringe pixels and nearly invisible alpha dust were removed. Gate outputs were resized to their specified dimensions. Images contain no text, numbers, birds, characters, or UI. The board has six left and six right birch perches; lakeside scenery includes willows, birches, reeds, lilies, and reflections.

**Strict geometry acceptance is not passed.** Repeated image-tool edits did not reproduce guide coordinates exactly. At x=250, left branch spans are approximately 568–575, 704–711, 841–846, 971–980, 1107–1114, and 1236–1249; the guide bar centers are approximately 570, 709, 849, 988, 1128, and 1267. Lower branches therefore remain too high. The initial painting is retained because later correction attempts introduced larger deviations.

The empty image was generated by editing the branched image, but regeneration altered pixels outside the removed branches (99.84% of pixels in the top 500 rows differ at least slightly). It preserves the overall composition, but does **not** meet the brief's literal unchanged-pixel requirement. The night painting preserves the overall arrangement but also redraws detail.

The map retains the same sequence and general placement of switchbacks, but its path is not pixel-exact to the scaled world 3 map. Its upper plain-grass band is only approximately 68 pixels rather than the requested 80. Gate framing is similar, but silhouettes are not identical to the world 3 set; open and closed arch detail also differs slightly. The backdrop has minor generated edge irregularities. These remain review defects, not claims of exact compliance.

## Prompt set

Shared direction: cozy, soft, bright polished cartoon painting matching the named references; willows and white birches around a calm blue lake, water lilies, reeds, gentle reflections; no text, numbers, birds, characters, or UI.

- Board: reference guide plus snow/green style references; twelve thin birch perches at the specified guide bars, calm sky and middle.
- Empty: remove only twelve perches and their tip leaves from the board; preserve everything else.
- Night: relight the empty image with moonlit blue, moon and subtle stars; preserve composition; snow-night painting as lighting reference.
- Map: repaint world 3 scenery into lakeside vegetation and water; preserve cream path and switchbacks; plain top/bottom 80 pixels.
- Frame: re-theme the world 3 arch with willow leaves, reeds and white lilies, preserving silhouette and transparent opening.
- Closed arch: same lakeside frame with lower-half honey wooden double doors and centered gold padlock, matching world 3 framing.
- Left/right doors: separate painted honey wooden leaves with matching curved tops, rails, golden studs, correct hinge side and small willow accents.
- Light: elongated transparent portal matching the reference, blue lake and willow silhouettes with warm golden rays and winding reflection.
- Backdrop: lakeside view in the reference's central rectangle, surrounding canvas transparent.
