# Bright cartoon dialog materials

The user explicitly protected the Sunny Aviary detail page and rejected the large dark green, textured Family popup container. The two original feedback images were inspected. This is a targeted unfinished Family container redesign, not a request to redraw Aviary or retained artwork.

## Contract

The seven Family dialogs keep all content, title and gold ordinals, bird positions and animation, rating stars, bottom actions, outside-frame Close and behavior. Their exterior reuses the existing `background_aviary_forest` pixels. The independent new `panel_dialog_cartoon` asset is a blank rounded panel with a thick glossy orange-gold/cream edge and clean saturated green field. It excludes photorealistic grain, bamboo, engraved foliage and dark desaturated teal. Its field must support the retained cream counters and body wording without new labels, shader outlines or badges.

The complete independent frame asset must pass alpha, edge, palette, empty-center and nine-patch checks before implementation. New artwork is generated; accepted images are not cropped into replacements or overwritten. Borders use fixed-scale corners across all container heights. Shared ShopSkin, protected Aviary and Home, maps, gameplay, and purchasing remain untouched unless separately assigned.

## Verification

Run all Family states in both locales, the full-roster Level 100 and long English names at native 1080×1920, actual action and Close regression, and the project's aggregate. Check the new material at the smallest and largest panel rectangles, and verify all retained image hashes. Review actual screenshots independently before integration; user visual acceptance remains pending.
