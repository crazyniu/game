# Visible skills — spec

User report (2026-11-14): "I do not know what each bird's skill is for." Each species' homecoming gift
(`Rules.GIFT_BY_SPECIES`) was explained once in the new-friend popup and nowhere during play. No rule changes.

1. **Bird card on demand.** A finger held on a bird for 0.45 s opens its card (`Popups.goal_info`). The press still
   selects as before; the hold drops that selection. Eggs do not answer.
2. **The card says when.** Between the bird and its ability line the card shows four of its faces, an arrow and
   the birdhouse: "when four go home". Birds without an effect (crow) have no such row.
3. **Before a level.** Every bird ring on the level card is tappable and opens the same card.
4. **Cause before effect.** When a flock goes home, its ability badge pops out of the birdhouse and a small badge
   flies to each thing it changes (sleepers, eggs, crows, the grown branch, the sun or moon, the move counter);
   the change plays when the badge arrives. Gifts with nothing to point at keep the old rise-and-fade.
