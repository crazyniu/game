# All-page bilingual text review

This is the preserved pre-cartoon-material checkpoint review. See [the current feedback review](feedback-review.md) for affected-state recaptures and remaining gates.

## Coverage and method

Reviewed all 92 distinct non-gameplay and instructional-overlay states exposed by the seven canonical preview builders in English and Chinese: 184 actual rendered frames. The canonical default names `coin_shop_view:normal` and `packages` refer to the same page; `packages` is used in this inventory. The gameplay-only board previews and transitional `leaf_transition` are explicitly not assessed. Tutorial/goal overlays are read-only review scope, not authority to modify gameplay.

Three separate questions were reviewed in actual images:
1. Is the title plaque, action or combined group centered relative to its owning page/control?
2. Is visible lettering centered within that plaque/face, ignoring transparent texture padding?
3. Is the complete icon-plus-number or word-plus-number union balanced, and is its outline legible without an extra surround?

The earlier 230-label diagnostic only measures text against its own assigned box and cannot answer questions 1 or 3. It did not detect the original Aviary sign at x496 or the Family ordinal composite offset. It is not used as an all-page pass.

All 184 frames were inspected in labeled rendered contact sheets; concerning cases were opened at full resolution, including both level 100 title groups. Left-aligned prose/option rows and clipped carousel/scroll neighbors remain intentional, not centering defects. This is a visual text review, not human aesthetic approval or animation/device/audio verification.

## Results and findings

- **Aviary:** original title/sign, header, ability and ownership/price defects corrected locally. All canonical list/detail/ownership/purchase states were recaptured from integrated code; the prior 10-species bilingual evidence supplements them.
- **Family F2:** this review found `Heading.refresh` used a word slot wider than the visible Level word. The combined Level+ordinal union was 16.3026px right in English and 10.0753px right in Chinese for 1/12/100. The owner corrected only the composite translation without changing art, dimensions or spacing. Independent postmerge measurement is 0 horizontal and at most 0.000004px vertical. All 6 Level-card frames were recaptured in new Godot processes after the fix; initial frames are retained only as build diagnostics.
- **N1 corrected in its authorized local scope:** the original Island glyph-cell layout left Shop price 100 about +1.65px right and purchase 1200 about +4.03px right. The purchase owner added local complete-ink translation for Shop prices, stock badges, balance rebuilds and Buy amounts, plus Aviary confirmation amounts. In the actual OpenGL test, 332 amount groups have maximum residual 0.0000153px X and 0.00000191px Y, with owning-control centering and unchanged textures/materials/sizes/spacing verified. All 36 affected canonical frames were recaptured from final integrated source. Shared renderers, protected pages, QuantityInput/caret metrics and outfit-owned fractions were not modified. Home/Map/Chest shared counters retain the previously documented cell-based numerical behavior; their visual review passed, not a new claim of mathematical zero offset.
- **Measurement isolation:** cross-page testing found that the previous Aviary softened-ink measurement could mutate a shared Image returned by get_image in headless Godot. The local helper now duplicates that Image before measuring. A valid original-function probe changes its in-memory hash and alpha bounds; the guarded probe preserves both. All ten digit images keep their byte hashes across the new Shop/Aviary, locale and value-change regression. Disk artwork is unchanged. The earlier single-preview-per-process visual audit did not cover this cross-page memory side effect. Six additional Aviary list/Sunny/Hoot frames verify that the defensive copy preserves previously delivered layout. Both lists are pixel-identical. For Sunny/Hoot, static header/hero/ability/title and bottom action strips are pixel-identical; differences are confined to lower live-outfit/cosmetic particles at different capture times, not a new layout. The complete detail-image hashes are therefore not claimed identical.
- **Outline review:** no additional excessive-outline defect found outside the corrected Aviary ownership/price treatment. Retained gold title outlines, normal action edges and disabled dimming are distinguished from adding duplicate outlines.

## Renderer diagnostic

The prior broad headless audit was not error-free. The exact error was `Condition "!actions.custom_samplers.has(function->arguments[j].tex_builtin)" is true` at `shader_compiler.cpp:1329`, emitted when Home assigns its existing icon-outline shader. That shader passes built-in TEXTURE through `sampler2D` arguments to `paint_at` and `silhouette`.

An isolated probe loads that unchanged shader and a real existing icon. Godot 4.7.2 `--headless --rendering-method gl_compatibility` reproduces the error with `DISPLAY=headless`, method gl_compatibility and driver opengl3. The same probe on X11 actual OpenGL 4.5 Mesa/llvmpipe Compatibility completes with no shader error. Actual Home captures also render normally. This establishes a backend-specific diagnostic in these two tested modes, not a general engine/platform guarantee. No protected shader was changed.

The fresh capture harness uses one canonical preview per process, isolated HOME/XDG paths, an explicit 1080x1920 viewport and frame-post-draw capture. This avoids the disposed-preview callbacks from the old multi-root diagnostic. All 144 initial fresh processes, 6 Level post-fix recaptures, 2 full-resolution replacements and 42 N1/final-Aviary recaptures exited 0, with no script/renderer ERROR lines in these canonical capture logs. This statement does not apply to other integration test runners, whose diagnostics are documented separately. The cloud driver emits its ordinary unsupported V-Sync warning. Early standalone probe setup failures are retained under build only and are not the comparison logs.
