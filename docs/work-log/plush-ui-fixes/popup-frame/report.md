## Implementation

- Shared `Body(Rect panel, string title, Rect titleRect, bool close, Rect closeRect)` uses absolute top-left design pixels. `GameUI.Local` converts to panel coordinates; `GameUI.Place` writes top-left anchors and the measured size. The original height overload remains compatible with the other popup owner. The daily-gift Edges frame uses its own measured panel and ribbon.
- Measured sprite crops remove source transparency without editing PNGs or import settings. Nine-sliced frames keep their borders at the intended visual weight. The layout JSONs specify 860 px frames, so those exact dimensions take precedence over the brief's approximate 780 px values.
- Win uses measured stars, threshold/move/reward pills, overlapping coin, replay/home discs and primary play disc. Replay uses `icon_replay`. The hero uses existing cheer frames with a clipped lower body instead of distorting the whole bird. Extra rays were removed. Infinity ink is enlarged to the authored text height.
- Settings uses the measured title, frame, close, controls and caption plates. Restart uses `icon_replay`. Disabled map actions retain their dimensions.
- Goal info uses a 431 px measured ring and the existing front animation enlarged to the measured bird bounds. The ability disc and caption stay inside the frame. A 20 px adjustment accommodates the retained caption artwork without covering the first letter.
- Level card uses `label_title_level_<N>`, the tall measured frame, thresholds, a single Best pill with `label_word_best`, a grid of portrait slots and ability coins, and a mechanic strip. Best is absent without a record. Empty roster slots stay empty. The actual level roster, thresholds and mechanics are preserved. The map HUD remains visible beneath the dim layer.
- `hoot_sleep_bare` was already a Sprite with a valid tracked meta. `GameArt.Optional` misclassified its `hoot_` prefix and looked under `Birds/`. The explicit Art-route exception fixes all failing level-card states; no import-file change was needed.
- There is no `SettingsPanel.cs` in this checkout; Settings lives in the assigned `PanelsView.Settings` method. Only the seven listed source/data files and this documentation folder are committed.

## Remaining differences and work for other owners

- The supplied `panel_wood`, stars, button glyphs, ribbon labels and caption plates differ from the mockup's painted shapes, fur, corners and letter proportions. The existing files are placed at the measured bounds. Matching replacements are needed for exact painted-subregion correspondence.
- The Win mockup's frontal, wide-wing hero is absent as an animated sprite. The retained Sunny cheer sequence is the closest moving replacement; its gaze and silhouette differ. Front portrait frames also differ from the mockup's tighter face artwork. Existing bird sequences remain under their canonical `Birds/` resource root; they were reused, never moved or regenerated.
- The exact split sun/moon mechanic disc is missing. `gift_coin_nightcall` is the closest existing day/night badge. The egg includes a question mark and its own nest; the existing sleeping owl and gold sleep marks differ from the mockup. Level 40 also has a growing-branch mechanic, shown in a separate small slot at the right of the strip.
- Mockup threshold 16 differs from the current authored `par + 3` threshold 15 at par 12. The runtime keeps the actual rule. Level 40's real species roster also differs from the decorative mockup roster.
- Board composition, house/goal HUD, toolbar, map scenery/navigation, and the map title “Forest Trail” versus “Green Forest” require the corresponding owners. They were not replaced with a static mockup backdrop.
- Edges / Zero coins and Maximum coins share the corrected frame, but gift size, opened lid, reward composition and missing surrounding coins remain owned by the popup-content worker. Their 0 px frame result does not validate the gift composition.
