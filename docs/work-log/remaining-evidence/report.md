# Remaining animation evidence

## Status

**Partial; issue #58 is not accepted.** The subsequent close-up evidence below resolves the day/night, hatch, Chorus, wake-up, branch-tip, reverse leaf, notification-driven gate and listed button-feedback gaps. The level 6 same-species landing wobble remains unproven: no such event was reachable in the transfer and effect-booster search described below. The shop card lacks press feedback ([#83](https://github.com/crazyniu/game/issues/83)).

## Method

Unity 6000.6.7f1, actual Game scene in PlayMode, graphics enabled, `Random.InitState(12345)`, `Time.captureDeltaTime = 1/60`, normal motion during each observation. Captures use a 540×960 camera target. Each JPEG is 900 px wide or less, except the 1080 px family detail sheet, quality 80. Each tile is labelled with actual elapsed `Time.time` in seconds; the matching CSV records scheduled and actual elapsed times. Game-time samples are not wall-clock performance measurements. The normal sampling interval is 0.1 s; button samples are 0.05 s and `family_flight` is consecutive 1/60 s samples. The temporary PlayMode fixture and generated metadata were removed and are not committed.

The fixture loaded authored levels through `LevelLoader` and used a nonpersistent 500-coin profile with booster stock. Instant motion was used to set up settled starting boards and was turned off before recording each action; it was also used for explicitly noted preceding moves. Actions were generally invoked through game methods, not physical input. Popup presentation was invoked directly, not produced by a full progression/reward transaction. The aviary collection was seeded in memory. The map gate was opened directly; it was not reached through an actual world-completion receipt. The capture sampled rendered camera frames, not every possible device frame. The requested `docs/work-log/motion-evidence/spec.md` does not exist on this tip or in local Git history; the method above follows the existing `docs/work-log/motion-evidence/report.md` and the issue description.

## Subsequent close-up evidence

The `*-closeup`, `egg-hatch`, `chorus-ribbon`, `bird-wake`, `branch-tip`, `reverse-leaf`, `world-*-flow`, and `button-*` files in `evidence/` supersede the corresponding limitations above. The temporary PlayMode captures rendered the actual Game scene to 1080×1920, then cropped at native pixel size. JPEG sheets are quality 80, at most 1440 px wide, with actual game-time elapsed on each tile. Frames are 0.05 s apart; the button CSVs additionally record every 1/60 s frame. The CSVs describe the sampled changes. Game time is not a performance measurement.

- **Egg hatch, level 6:** `egg-hatch.jpg` shows the mint flock depart, the exposed egg crack at 0.75 s, the shell at 0.90 s, and the mint bird emerge at 0.95 s. A tutorial hand enters at 1.10 s. This is not a wobble capture. A non-Unity breadth-first search of the authored level visited 520 distinct states and 3,932 legal normal-transfer actions without an `EggWobbled` event. Including one each of Sapling, Owl Eye, Morning Bell, Scarecrow and Spring Rain visited 4,928 distinct states and 48,746 legal actions without that event; Firefly was excluded because it does not rearrange the board. The starting mint egg is beneath all three remaining mint birds, and moving that run off the branch exposes and hatches it. The search does not prove reachability under modified level data or arbitrary injected state.
- **Day/night, level 33:** `day_night_closeup.jpg` samples 15.50–17.00 s following 0→6 (instant setup) and 1→0 (normal motion); `day_night_closeup.csv` records the entire 0–21 s playback. The long homecoming cascade delays presentation: at 15.80 s the dial still shows sun and 1, at 15.85 s it switches to moon and 2, and the sky progressively darkens through 16.45 s. The scene night-overlay alpha rises from 0 to 1 and the action becomes idle at 21.00 s. The earlier 15 s observation ended before the transition; it is not evidence of a day/night defect.
- **Chorus:** `chorus-ribbon.jpg` shows an earned x2 green ribbon appearing at approximately 3.35 s after the second level 1 homecoming, expanding, remaining briefly, then becoming darkened by the win presentation before disappearing. The first move was instant setup; the second and ribbon used normal motion.
- **Wake and growth:** `bird-wake.jpg` shows a level 11 sleeping berry open its eyes after Morning Bell and later depart with its flock. `branch-tip.jpg` shows a level 31 short growing branch extend across successive frames after Spring Rain, with a sparkle near the tip. These are sampled views, not a device-level audit.
- **Leaf and notifications:** `reverse-leaf.jpg` shows level-to-map leaves covering the level at approximately 0.15–0.25 s and revealing the map by 0.45 s, with notifications enabled. `world-complete-flow.jpg`, `world-gate-flow.jpg`, and `world-unlock-flow.jpg` show the actual level 10 win notification path: world completion, acknowledgement through its real button, map scroll and unobstructed world 2 gate opening, then the world-unlock portal. Unrelated daily and new-friend receipts were seeded. The gate reports opening at 4.75–5.60 s; the unlock was not acknowledged in this capture. The gate sheet changes crop at 6.00 s to follow the unlock popup. This verifies the displayed sequence, not persistence or physical touch.
- **Buttons:** `button-{undo,hint,branch,level-booster,settings,close,replay,next,home,shop,aviary,shop-card,shop-confirm}.jpg` and matching CSVs record EventSystem-dispatched pointer down at 0.05 s and up at 0.20 s. All sampled disc controls shrink to local scale 0.92, then ease back with a 1.00792 overshoot; their recorded group and graphic alpha remain 1. The shop Firefly card stays at scale 1 and alpha 1 throughout, with no visible pressed feedback: [#83](https://github.com/crazyniu/game/issues/83). These are dispatched pointer handlers, not physical touch or raycast acceptance. The first undo tile was captured before its graphic appeared; the later tiles and per-frame measurements cover the press and release.
