## 2. After level 01 review: prefer A, evaluate only one small variation

Official descriptions below were rechecked on 2026-10-31; none was installed or interaction-tested for this note. Sources establish their stated mechanic, not our exact capacity, unlock trigger, layout or solution. These are future review briefs, not permission to implement all three.

### A — Preferred: short relay branches beside completion branches

**Source facts:** [Gameware Nut Sort Mania, Features / How to play](https://www.gamewareeurope.com/Public/games?id=nutsort) explicitly uses short, medium and long rods, moving top-color nuts to an empty or same-top-color rod. [Bird Sort 2, HOW TO PLAY](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.globalplay.birdsort2.color.puzzle&hl=en-US) says a sorted flock flies away and its branch is lost.

**Our proposal:** combine mixed relay capacities with the existing completion-only retirement rule. A short branch would transfer birds but could not hold a complete flock; exact capacities and puzzles remain unapproved. The decision to test is whether to preserve a long branch for completion or use it for relay, and when a short receiver forces a partial transfer. Neither source proves this combined design is fun.

**Required changes before adoption:** revise RULES' uniform capacity contract; add explicit per-branch capacity to schema/state/hash/save/validation; update transfer/solver keys and completion checks without retiring a merely emptied relay; show actual slots and legal landing capacity in UI. Free assistance semantics must be reviewed, not guessed.

**Concrete validation brief, not a supplied solution:** author one fixed N4/K3 candidate with exactly A4/B4/C4 total, at least one proposed short relay and long completion positions. Freeze the entire initial array first, then validate a complete unassisted route plus the preserve-long-space/partial-relay counterfactual. No array, minimum move count or successful witness is asserted here.

### B — Backup: key-linked rescue

**Source fact:** [Kunhar Magic Bird Sort, Box Unlock Challenge](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kunhargames.bird.sort.game.color.puzzle&hl=en) states that players collect keys to unlock hidden birds. It does not disclose which flock grants a key or the exact unlock timing.

**Our proposal:** a clearly linked flock-completion key could open a specific rescue location. That trigger/link is our proposal, not verified competitor behavior; it is not a reinstatement of old RevealAfter/RevealSpecies formulas.

**Decision / dependencies:** choose which accessible group to complete first to release blocked birds. Adoption needs approved key ownership/consumption/target semantics, schema and Undo/save changes, solver lock state, and clear key-to-lock UI/events. Validation brief: a fixed N4/K3 puzzle with A4/B4/C4 including rescued birds; demonstrate an accessible key route and no self-locking dependency, then replay without assistance. Exact layout and route remain unvalidated.

### C — Reserve: inbound-only collector

**Source fact:** [BOOPFUN Water Sort, WHAT'S INSIDE](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.boopfun.watersort) describes collector bottles that accept liquid but cannot pour it out. Its color binding, exact capacity and completed-container behavior are not disclosed.

**Our proposal / decision:** evaluate committing a group to an inbound-only bird receiver versus retaining reversible space. Do not assume its full flock flies, retires, clears or binds a color. Before adoption, specify those missing boundaries and update RULES/schema, legal-source generation and solver keys, Undo/save, and an unmistakable one-way UI cue. Validation brief: one fixed, conserved N4/K3 candidate containing A4/B4/C4, with a fully defined collector and complete unassisted replay; no candidate or witness is claimed yet.
