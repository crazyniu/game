Paint six standalone home-screen menu icons for a cozy mobile bird puzzle game, as ONE sheet on a fully transparent
background (real alpha), laid out in a clean grid of 3 columns x 2 rows with generous empty space between them.
Reading order: 1 Daily Challenge, 2 Spin, 3 Map, 4 Shop, 5 Aviary, 6 Chest.

Each icon is a single self-contained painted object, NO frame, NO button plate, NO border, NO tile, NO text, NO letters,
NO numbers anywhere. Each object is about 700 px wide, centred in its grid cell, with a soft thin cream-white sticker rim
around its silhouette and a small soft contact shadow directly under it (the shadow is part of the object, nothing else
touches the background). Style: soft plush, felt-and-fleece storybook look like the game's fluffy birds, gently rounded
forms, matte, calm natural colours (not over-bright, no neon), warm light from the upper left. All six share the same
visual weight, the same lighting and the same rim, so they read as one set.

Icons:
1 Daily Challenge: a tear-off desk calendar page with a small yellow chick sitting on top of it and a little red flag stuck in the page, one golden sparkle.
2 Spin: a round prize wheel seen almost front-on, divided into 6 soft wedges in green, cream, coral, gold, blue and
  pink, with a gold hub and a small red pointer on top, two tiny sparkles.
3 Map: a rolled parchment treasure map with a dotted red path and a red location pin.
4 Shop: a cute red-and-cream striped shop bag with a golden handle and a little smiling chick face on it.
5 Aviary: a woven straw nest holding three speckled pink eggs with two green leaves.
6 Chest: a wooden treasure chest with golden bands and a lock, lid closed.

Output: the single sheet only, transparent background, at least 2000 px wide.
