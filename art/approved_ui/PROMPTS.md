## Style bible (include the relevant parts in EVERY prompt)
- **Look**: premium casual mobile game, 3D-rendered toy / candy look, soft studio lighting, every UI object is a chunky inflated glossy object with real volume: bright specular highlight on the top-left, soft glow on the rim, darker saturated bottom edge, thick dark-brown outline (about 6-8 px at 1080 wide), soft drop shadow underneath. Saturated, warm, happy colors. NO flat rectangles, NO thin lines, NO pale cream web cards, NO tiny text, NO generic app/dashboard look.
- **Materials**: warm honey-brown wood with visible grain and rope lashings; polished brass rims; glossy gem-like colored buttons (green, gold, blue, purple, red); leafy trims; parchment only as small inner panels. Panels = chunky carved wooden frames with leaf corners and a rope, NOT flat cards.
- **Buttons**: big glossy round gem buttons (about 150 px) with a thick gold-brown ring; the icon is a BOLD white pictogram with a thick dark outline and drop shadow (never a thin line icon). Primary action = big green gem button. Small count bubbles are red glossy circles with a white number.
- **Typography**: chunky rounded display font (Lilita One / Fredoka One style), white or cream fill, thick dark-brown outline, hard drop shadow, optional slight italic for banners. Numbers are big and bold. Keep real text to a minimum: level number, coin amount, prices, names on name ribbons.
- **Characters (ours, large and fluffy, filling the screen)**: Sunny (round lemon-yellow chick with one curly tuft and rosy cheeks), Skye (sky-blue egg-shaped bird, white belly, round top-knot bun, calm half-closed eyes), Berry (raspberry-magenta bird with two long swept-back head plumes, wink), Minty (mint-teal bird with a leaf-shaped crest and round eye rings), Cocoa (chocolate-brown owl-like bird with a cream face mask and ear tufts), Flurry (white bird with lilac wing tips and a three-dot lilac crown), Ember (orange bird with a flame-shaped crest), Inky (navy bird with a white star on the chest), Hoot (pale tawny owl chick with a heart-shaped face disc and a star nightcap), Rook (slate-grey crow with a crooked smile). Mystery egg = speckled cream egg in a twig nest with a soft question mark.
- **Composition rule for gameplay screens**: the birds are the stars. Each bird is about 200 px wide on a 1080 px screen, birds sit shoulder to shoulder on thick tree branches (branches about 60 px thick, rows only about 240 px apart), left branches grow from the left screen edge and right branches from the right edge with leafy bushes at the roots, the board fills most of the screen. HUD is small and sparse: coin pill top-left, one big chunky wooden-and-red-roof goal birdhouse hanging at top center (or several smaller ones when there are many species), glossy gear button top-right, a row of big round gem power-up buttons at the bottom. Soft bokeh forest background, rim light, a few sparkles and glowing trails for the flying birds.
- Output: portrait 9:16 game screenshot look, 1080x1920 after resizing. No watermark, no device frame, no extra marketing text.
