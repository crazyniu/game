## New artwork, QA, and deviations

- Seven built-in image-tool calls produced four independent houses, one nine-cell accessory sheet, one nest gift, and an optional accessory-background cleanup variant. The original accessory sheet's real alpha was already usable and had less residual low-alpha background than the cleanup variant, so final composition uses the original; both are preserved in `raw/`. Tool previews showed RGB outside transparent pixels, which was checked against the actual alpha channel before selection.
- Fourteen transparent derivatives are retained: four houses, six cosmetic accessories, hanger, crossed hanger, and nest gift. Slicing sets alpha below 16 to zero and crops to remaining alpha bounds. No original asset is overwritten. [qa/sprite_metrics.json](qa/sprite_metrics.json) records dimensions, alpha extrema, transparent-pixel counts and bounds. Center `(0.5,0.5)` is only a composition pivot; Unity pivots/import settings remain unaccepted.
- [qa/recognition_sheet.png](qa/recognition_sheet.png) shows 128 px color, 64 px color, 64 px grayscale, and 44 px grayscale views. Designer inspection finds the four house silhouettes and accessory categories distinguishable at 64 px; scarf color variants intentionally share a silhouette. Tiny nest coins and the Encore badge's crossed-number detail require their larger information context. This is not a user recognition study.
- [qa/contact_sheet.png](qa/contact_sheet.png) shows all eleven screens. [qa/validation.json](qa/validation.json) records dimensions, final hashes, and explicit runtime/animation exclusions. [qa/source_hashes.json](qa/source_hashes.json) verifies that every source read by the final composition remained unchanged during it.
- The six species share a scarf design and some other accessories to demonstrate layout/state treatment; production per-species anchors, every pose, multiple simultaneously equipped outfits, and a full cosmetic catalog are outside this mockup delivery.
- Names use the v5 species table: **Skye, Minty, Flurry, Inky, Rook**. Header balances, inventories, ownership, home counts, and met/unmet states are display examples, not captured save data. Bundle prices and +20/+50/+200 rewards come from the task's designated source.
- The T45 scope is conceptual art only. No game code, runtime assets/data, scenes, import settings, owning specifications, commits, or publications were changed by this worker. Interactions, purchases, equip persistence, localization, safe areas, touch targets, Unity rendering/tests, runtime footage, and real animation remain **NOT_RUN**.
